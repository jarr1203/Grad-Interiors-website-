# Gradinteriors

Build a complete, premium, fully responsive portfolio website for an interior designer, using React, Tailwind CSS, and shadcn/ui components. Single-page layout with smooth-scroll navigation.

## ATTACHED ASSETS
- I've attached my LOGO: use it in the navbar, footer, and as the favicon. Pick the color palette from the logo's colors (one accent color plus warm neutrals).
- I've attached PROJECT IMAGES: use them in the hero and the portfolio gallery. Don't use stock photos where my images can go. Only use clearly marked placeholders if more images are needed.

## BRAND & TONE
- Business name: Grad Interiors
- Tagline: "Redefining Your Space"
- Style: elegant, warm, editorial, like a high-end interior design magazine. Lots of white space, large imagery.
- Fonts: Playfair Display (headings) + Inter (body), loaded from Google Fonts.
- Subtle animations: fade-in on scroll, image zoom on hover, smooth transitions.

## LOCATIONS
- I serve Bangalore and Tamil Nadu, covering all of South India.
- Only ever mention "Bangalore" and "Tamil Nadu" as locations. Do not mention any individual city names anywhere on the site.

## SECTIONS (in order)
1. **Sticky navbar**: logo, links (Home, Portfolio, Why Choose Me, Reviews, Contact), "Book a Consultation" button. Hamburger menu on mobile.
2. **Hero**: full-screen background using my best attached image, headline, subheading, buttons "View Portfolio" and "Book a Free Consultation", plus the line "200+ Projects Completed Across South India".
3. **About**: short intro to me as the designer and my design philosophy, with space for my photo.
4. **Portfolio gallery**: masonry/grid of ALL my attached images, with category filter tabs (Living Room, Bedroom, Kitchen, Office, Full Home) and a lightbox with next/previous. Each image gets a short caption and a location label of either "Bangalore" or "Tamil Nadu".
5. **Why Choose Me**: 6 icon cards (lucide-react icons):
   - 200+ projects delivered
   - Personalized designs for your lifestyle and budget
   - Transparent pricing, no hidden costs
   - On-time delivery
   - End-to-end service, from concept to handover
   - Quality materials and trusted vendors
6. **Services**: Residential Interiors, Commercial Interiors, Modular Kitchens, Space Planning, Custom Furniture, Renovation.
7. **Service Areas**: highlight that I serve Bangalore and Tamil Nadu, covering all of South India. Show just two location badges, "Bangalore" and "Tamil Nadu".
8. **Animated stats counters**: 200+ Projects, 12+ Years
9. **Reviews**: a testimonials section with client reviews (use tasteful placeholder quotes clearly meant to be replaced with real client reviews).
10. **Contact / Book a Consultation**: a consultation booking form (name, phone, email, project type, message) with a visible note that details will be confirmed over a call. Plus business contact details area with placeholders for phone, email, WhatsApp, and Instagram links.
11. **Footer**: logo, quick links, locations "Bangalore" and "Tamil Nadu" only, and social icons.

Note: the attached markdown prompt was cut off at the stats counters section; sections 9-11 above complete the page to match the navbar links (Reviews, Contact).
